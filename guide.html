Beginner Guide

The few things that matter most in your first three weeks.

How to spend your early resources

  • Give your LMD to promotions first, rather than spreading it evenly across every operator.
  • Don't rush to pull with your premium currency — save it for a guarantee event.
  • Sanity regenerates every day, so spend it down to nothing before you sleep.

Common beginner mistakes

Mistake one: sprinkling resources over every operator, so none of them can hold the mid-game main story.

Mistake two: chasing high-cost operators too early, running out of DP at the start and getting overrun in the first two waves.

Mistake three: ignoring deployment order — putting the medic behind the DPS, so the front line falls before the medic is even down.

Recommended raising order

First make sure every lane has someone who can block; only then worry about your damage ceiling. A squad that holds its ground reliably is worth more than a pile of high-rarity operators that don't fit together. Build in this order:

  • First: Amiya — arts damage, useful through the early and mid main story.
  • Second: Exusiai — clears ground enemies and saves you deployment slots.
  • Third: Shining — single-target healing that keeps the front line standing.
  • Fourth: Siege — frontline blocking with DP recovery.
