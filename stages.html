Stages

Three main-story stages that tend to stop people, and how to get through them.

1-7

Piercing Arrow

Placeholder summary: the first early stage that really asks for crowd control — enemies keep pushing down from above.

How to clear it

  1. Open by putting ranged damage on the centre high ground, covering both lanes.
  2. Put a blocker on the left to hold until your medic is in place.
  3. Save DP for the medic before the second wave — don't rush a third damage dealer.

RecommendedAmiya, Exusiai, Shining

2-10

Greenwater Riverside

Placeholder summary: narrow terrain, with enemies flanking in two waves from the sides.

How to clear it

  1. Learn where the two spawn points are, and hold the rear side first.
  2. Armoured units show up mid-stage, so you need arts damage ready to take over.
  3. Keep one deployment slot free before the last wave, to plug any leaks.

RecommendedSilverAsh, Eyjafjalla, Siege

4-9

Land of Gratitude

Placeholder summary: a mid-to-late all-round stage that tests the stability of your squad rather than its burst.

How to clear it

  1. Build the healing and blocking skeleton first; worry about damage ceilings afterwards.
  2. Don't spend all your DP at once — leave room to react to a leak.
  3. If the front line keeps breaking, swap in a sturdier blocker instead of adding damage.
  4. Three stars beats a perfect clear: pass it first, optimise later.

RecommendedShining, Siege, SilverAsh, Amiya
